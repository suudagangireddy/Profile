The Problem

Enterprise teams needed polished output without knowing how to design or code.

As Brane's no-code platform generated enterprise apps, businesses also needed branded websites, invoice templates, email communications, and reports. But they had no designers or developers to create them.

My task: design a visual CMS where a business analyst with zero design knowledge could build production-ready, branded output in minutes.

Output types needed

Websites Invoices Email templates Reports Certificates

Design Constraint

Must work for users with zero design or development background — a business analyst should be fully self-sufficient.

Design Approach

Markdown-first, visual-first, output-first

01

Markdown-based content model

Using markdown as the content foundation meant templates were portable, versionable, and could render to any output format — HTML, PDF, or email — from the same source. A key architectural decision that shaped the entire UX.

02

Responsive-first component design

Every component was designed mobile-first, with responsive behavior built in. Users could build once and have their templates adapt across devices without writing a single media query.

03

Dual-mode: DIV and static page creation

The platform supported both a dynamic DIV-based approach for complex, data-driven layouts and a static page mode for simpler designs — giving users the right tool without forcing a choice upfront.
