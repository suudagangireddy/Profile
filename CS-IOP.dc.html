India Operations Platform — Vendor Management for CBRE

Designing a unified internal platform for CBRE D&T India to streamline vendor hiring, onboarding, and contract tracking — replacing fragmented spreadsheets and email threads.

Role

Lead Product Designer

Client

CBRE D&T India

Type

Internal Enterprise Tool

Contractors tracked

125+ vendors

01 The problem

Managing 125+ contractors across multiple vendors shouldn't require a spreadsheet and three email threads.

CBRE's India D&T team managed vendor partnerships, contractor hiring, and MSA compliance through a patchwork of tools — Excel for headcount, email for onboarding, and calendar alerts for contract expiry dates. There was no single source of truth.

The result: expired contracts going unnoticed, onboarding delays, and ops managers spending hours reconciling data that should have been visible at a glance.

No unified view

Vendor data, contractor headcount, and MSA status lived in separate tools with no consolidated view for leadership.

Contract expiry blind spots

MSAs expired silently. No proactive alerting meant vendor relationships often lapsed before anyone noticed.

Friction in onboarding

New contractor onboarding relied on manual email chains between HR, ops, and the vendor — with no tracking or status visibility.

Authentication — supports both CBRE SSO and external vendor user login

02 Design decisions

One platform. Three workflows. Zero context switching.

D. 01

Dashboard as ops command center

The vendor dashboard surfaces the most time-sensitive information first — expiring MSAs, contractor headcount changes, and top vendor distribution — so ops managers know where to act without digging.

D. 02

Proactive over reactive

Expiring MSA table shows days remaining with color-coded urgency, so contract renewals happen before they lapse — not after a vendor relationship has quietly expired.

D. 03

Dual access model

Internal CBRE staff use SSO; external vendors log in separately. Permissions and data visibility are scoped per role, so each party only sees what's relevant to them without separate systems.

D. 04

Timesheet integration

Contractor timesheets live in the same platform as vendor tracking — closing the loop between headcount data and actual billing, and removing yet another external tool from the ops workflow.

03 Outcome

Launched internally across CBRE D&T India, replacing fragmented ops tools with one platform.

The platform went live in early 2025, consolidating vendor management, contractor onboarding, MSA tracking, and timesheets into a single tool used daily by the D&T India operations team.

Designed within CBRE's enterprise design system and security requirements, shipped with SSO, role-based access, and full audit trail support.
