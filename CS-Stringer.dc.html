The Problem

Enterprises needed a secure AI workspace — without sending data to third-party LLMs.

With tools like ChatGPT becoming integral to daily work, companies faced a critical dilemma: these tools were powerful but sent sensitive business data to external servers.

My task: design a platform that felt as natural as a consumer chat app — but ran entirely on a private, company-owned LLM with no external data exposure.

Pain Points

ChatGPT sending sensitive company data to OpenAI servers

Communication, AI, and project tracking in 3 separate tools

No enterprise-grade permissions, broadcasting, or workflows

AI couldn't access internal company context and documents

My Brief

Design an enterprise communication platform with a private LLM at its core — secure, intelligent, and as intuitive as WhatsApp.

Design Decisions

Making AI feel native, not bolted on

Familiar chat paradigm, enterprise depth

The interface was modeled on apps users already loved — then layered with enterprise features like broadcast, sprint integration, and AI context at exactly the right moments.

LLM context as a first-class UI element

Rather than a sidebar AI, I designed Stringer so the LLM had access to conversation history, documents, and user context — and the UI made that intelligence visible and trustworthy.

Permission clarity without friction

In enterprises, who can see what matters. I designed group and permission flows to be simple to use but powerful — right access for PMs, devs, and stakeholders without complexity.

Feedback built into communication

The feedback system was designed inside the communication flow — not a separate tool — so teams could surface issues and close loops without leaving the conversation.
