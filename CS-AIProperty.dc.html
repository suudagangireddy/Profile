AI Property — Smart Home Discovery That Understands Your Lifestyle

A consumer PropTech app that uses AI to match buyers and renters to homes based on lifestyle compatibility — not just filters and square footage.

Role

Lead Product Designer

Platform

iOS · Mobile-first

Category

Consumer · PropTech

Onboarding

< 2 minutes

01 The problem

Property search is broken. It finds homes that fit filters, not people that fit lives.

Every property platform gives you the same interface: bedrooms, bathrooms, price range, square footage. The filters are transactional. They can't capture what actually makes a home right — proximity to your gym, a quiet street for remote work, a neighborhood that fits your social rhythm.

The result: buyers browse dozens of technically-matching properties and still feel like nothing fits. The search is efficient but the discovery experience is deeply unsatisfying.

Filter fatigue

Traditional search returns hundreds of technically-matching listings with no way to understand which ones actually fit your life.

No lifestyle signal

Platforms capture what a property has, not what a person needs — walkability, community feel, commute stress, and neighborhood energy are invisible in search results.

Cold discovery

Browsing is transactional — no explanations, no context, no sense of why a listing appears. Every property feels equally relevant and equally uncertain.

02 Design decisions

Three mechanics that make AI feel like a knowledgeable friend, not a recommendation engine.

D. 01

AI Lifestyle Onboarding

A conversational intake — under 2 minutes — that captures what the user actually values: commute tolerance, social preferences, work-from-home needs, exercise habits. The profile drives matching, not filters.

D. 02

Smart Match Scores

Every listing shows a match score with a plain-language explanation — "Great for remote workers, quiet street, 8-min walk to your gym type." AI reasoning is surfaced, not hidden behind a number.

D. 03

Swipe Discovery

Tinder-style browsing with AI explanations on each card. Swipe signals train the model in real time — each decision makes the next recommendation sharper. Discovery is a feedback loop, not a one-time search.

03 Reflection

Rethinking search as a relationship, not a query.

The core design challenge was making AI's role visible without making it the hero. Users don't want to think about how the AI works — they want to feel understood. The match score and swipe mechanic give AI a tangible output without demanding the user trust a black box.

The onboarding was the hardest problem: gathering enough signal to make meaningful matches without feeling like a questionnaire. Conversational framing and sub-2-minute completion were non-negotiable design constraints.
