Curaway.ai — Agentic AI Health Concierge

Designing the end-to-end patient experience for a medical tourism platform powered by agentic AI — from first inquiry to post-treatment recovery.

01 The problem

Medical tourism promises world-class care at a fraction of the cost. The experience delivers chaos.

US healthcare costs are rising 3–5× faster than incomes. Wait times in Canada hit 27.7 weeks. 42M+ Americans remain uninsured. The alternative — seeking treatment abroad — saves 30–70% with no quality trade-off. But the journey to get there is broken at every step.

Over 60% of patients who explore medical travel abandon at the inquiry stage — not because the option is bad, but because navigating it alone is overwhelming, opaque, and anxiety-inducing.

Inquiry phase

The Trust Gap

No single source of truth. Unverified brokers. Information overload. 60%+ abandon here.

Planning phase

The Transparency Trap

Hidden costs, unpredictable pricing, visa and travel logistics managed in silos.

Treatment phase

The Anxiety Gap

Unverified provider quality. Cultural and language barriers at the point of care.

Recovery phase

The Aftercare Void

Zero coordination post-procedure. Disconnected records and no telehealth follow-up.

02 Design decisions

Four friction points. One design principle: replace uncertainty with confidence at every step.

D. 01

Journey-first, not feature-first

The product architecture mirrors the patient journey — Inquiry → Plan → Travel → Recover — so patients always know where they are and what comes next. Every feature maps to a moment of doubt we're eliminating.

D. 02

Trust through radical transparency

The price predictor shows itemized costs — procedure, travel, accommodation, aftercare — before any commitment. JCI-accreditation status and verified outcomes data are surfaced on every provider card, not buried in a PDF.

D. 03

50-factor matching, zero jargon

The matching engine weighs clinical, cultural, and emotional factors. The UX hides the complexity — patients answer a concierge-style intake wizard, not a medical form. The AI does the heavy lifting invisibly.

D. 04

Agentic handoffs, not form fills

Visa processing, flight booking, accommodation, and telehealth follow-up are handled by the AI agent — not delegated back to the user. The patient sees status, not tasks. The logistics layer is invisible by design.

03 The concierge interface

The AI feels like a knowledgeable friend, not a search engine.

The concierge chat is the primary interaction surface — patients describe their condition in plain language and receive curated options, not a list of hospitals to research themselves.

Designed for cross-platform parity — the mobile and web experiences are identical in capability, so patients can start a conversation on mobile and continue on desktop without losing context.

Tangled coordination without Curaway vs. unified orchestration with the platform

04 Outcome

A pitch-ready product design in a market growing at 23% CAGR.

Designed the full product experience — patient concierge app, provider-facing B2B analytics dashboard, and brand identity — to support fundraising and GTM execution across Phase 1 corridors (EU/US/UK/UAE → India/Turkey).

The design system was built for HIPAA, GDPR, and DPDP compliance from day one — trust is a product feature, not an afterthought.
