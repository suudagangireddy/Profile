Operating Model

How I approach design leadership

Practical frameworks refined over 13 years of leading product teams and partnering with engineering.

01 / DISCOVERY

Discovery in the open

I keep research and trade-offs visible. Early sketches, product assumptions, and edge cases are shared with engineering and product before polish begins.

02 / RATIONALE

Documented decisions

Every design decision comes with the 'why'. Documenting trade-offs preserves intent, speeds alignment, and helps teams adapt gracefully when constraints shift.

03 / IMPLEMENTATION

Implementation empathy

Designing with production reality in mind: defining tokens, empty states, error fallbacks, and boundary conditions so handoff is clear and build velocity stays high.

04 / CRAFT

Execution fidelity

Staying embedded through QA, visual verification, and analytics review to ensure the customer experience matches the intended design standard.
