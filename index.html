03 Why work with me?

Designers who can hold both ends — strategy and craft, domain depth and shipping — are rare.

I work best where the stakes are real, the systems are complex, and the users need things to just make sense.

A

I design from the lifecycle, not the screen.

I think about how people discover, learn, use, and grow with a product — then design the screens to support that arc, not just the happy path.

B

AI that earns trust, not just attention.

I've designed LLM interfaces, AI-generated UIs, and document intelligence products. I know when AI helps and when it gets in the way — and how to make the difference visible.

C

Density is not the enemy.

Enterprise UX lives in dense information. I don't simplify by removing what matters — I organize it so users can scan, understand, and act with confidence.

04 How I work?

What working together actually looks like.

H. 01

Discovery in the open

I keep discovery visible — sharing questions, tradeoffs, and early thinking before the design gets polished. Direction gets sharper when the team is involved from the start.

H. 02

Decisions documented, not just designed

I don't just design the final state. I make the reasoning visible — so decisions feel clear, intentional, and easier to build on when requirements change.

H. 03

Clear specs, fewer gaps

I design with implementation in mind — defining states, edge cases, and component specs so the work moves from design to build without constant back-and-forth.

H. 04

Support where it matters

I stay close to implementation — helping clarify interaction details, edge cases, and visual QA so the final product actually matches the intent of the design.
