Capital Markets Document Abstractor

AI-powered extraction and review experience for complex real estate documents — transforming unstructured offering memorandums, BOVs, and leases into structured, validated data.

Role

Lead UX Designer

Client

CBRE Capital Markets

Document types

BOV · OM · Lease · Rent Roll

Queue

248 documents

01 The problem

Capital Markets teams were losing days to manual document review. Every transaction depended on it.

Offering memorandums, Broker Opinion of Value documents, leases, and rent rolls contain dozens of critical data fields. Analysts were reading through them manually — extracting, cross-referencing, and entering data into systems by hand. On large deals, this took days.

The AI could extract the data. The missing piece was a review experience that gave analysts enough confidence to trust it — without making them feel like they were just approving a black box.

Manual extraction was the bottleneck

Analysts read entire documents to pull specific fields — a process that could span hours per document and was prone to transcription errors.

No traceability between data and source

Once data was entered into a system, there was no link back to the document page it came from. Errors were hard to catch and impossible to audit.

AI output without a trust framework

AI extraction existed but without confidence indicators or source references — analysts had no way to quickly know which values to verify and which to trust.

Queue List — 248 documents across Abstraction, Processing, Validation, Completed, and Error states

02 Design decisions

The review experience had to make AI confidence legible — not just present it.

D. 01

Split-panel with live source traceability

The review screen keeps the source document and extracted attributes side-by-side. Each field links to the exact page in the document where the AI found it — analysts can validate in seconds rather than hunting through a PDF.

D. 02

Confidence % as a triage tool

Every extracted field shows an AI confidence percentage. High-confidence fields (95–100%) can be scanned and approved. Low-confidence fields require review. This turns an 80-field document into a triage list — analysts focus only where it matters.

D. 03

Inline editing without context loss

Corrections happen in place — no navigation to a separate edit screen, no loss of document context. The source document stays visible while the analyst types. Bulk editing lets multiple fields be updated at once for repetitive corrections.

D. 04

Multi-state workflow queue

The queue tracks every document across Abstraction → Processing → Validation → Completed states, with Error handling surfaced prominently. Teams can see exactly where each document sits in the workflow without chasing colleagues for status.

03 CRM integration

Abstracted data surfaces directly inside ConnectorPro — where deals already live.

Rather than requiring analysts to switch between the Abstractor and the CRM, abstracted documents are surfaced inline within ConnectorPro opportunity records. The link between document, abstraction, and deal is maintained without duplication.

The "Abstract New" shortcut and document history panel were designed to make document abstraction a natural part of the existing deal workflow — not a parallel system to maintain.

04 Outcome

Analysts can now review, validate, and submit abstracted documents in a fraction of the time — with full source traceability.

The redesigned experience reduced cognitive load by surfacing the AI's reasoning (confidence scores, source page links) directly in the review workflow. Structured data presentation and inline editing removed the friction of switching between tools.

The design system patterns established for Document Abstractor — confidence states, validation workflows, AI-generated data indicators — created a reusable foundation for future CBRE AI products.
